# TP-Link TL-WR703N

- SoC: Atheros AR9331; MIPS 24Kc
- Typical configuration: 32 MiB RAM; 4 MiB SPI NOR flash
- M3: SoC map, UART, RAM, SPI NOR, interrupt controller and reset
- M4: U-Boot, Ethernet and MikroWrt boot
- Wi-Fi is explicitly out of scope for initial bring-up

Firmware images and flash layouts can vary by hardware revision; identify the exact board version before claiming image compatibility.
